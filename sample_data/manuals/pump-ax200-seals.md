# Axial Pump AX200 Mechanical Seal Guide (fictional)

Asset: PUMP-AX200
Document type: seals

## Purpose

This fictional guide covers mechanical seal leakage on PUMP-AX200. It does
not discuss CNC spindles or coolant loops.

## Symptoms

Seal weep, wet skid, and rising vibration at the pump bearing housing.
Temperature at the CNC spindle is not a diagnostic for this pump.

## Maintenance

Replace the mechanical seal cartridge and check flush water. Open a seal
replacement work order only after confirming leakage at the pump.

## Related codes

SEAL_LEAK, vibration, PUMP-AX200.
