# Conveyor B17 Belt Tracking Notes (fictional)

Asset: CONVEYOR-B17
Document type: belt

## Purpose

Belt tracking and tension for CONVEYOR-B17. Unrelated to coolant or TEMP_HIGH.

## Symptoms

Edge fray, tracking walk, and occasional belt-slip alarms. Spindle coolant
flow is not used on this conveyor.

## Maintenance

Re-track the belt and inspect pulleys. Do not open a CNC cooling work order
for conveyor noise.

## Related codes

BELT_SLIP, CONVEYOR-B17.
